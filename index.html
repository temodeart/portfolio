The apps I made for various reasons beside work.

  • Aise

    Menstrual cycle tracking for boyfriends. Not for girls.

  • Exit Plan

    A fake call app that saves you from an undesired situation.

  • Cattime

    A pomodoro app. During the break a big fat cute cat comes and sits on your screen, blocking you from working, then walks away when the break is over.

  • A Block Runner

    A Temple Run-like game, but with blocks. My first game, made in C# with the Unity engine.

  • The Lost Kitten

    An ongoing 2.5D horror project about a kitten lost in a big city, trying to find a way home.

  • The Lost Astronaut

    A psychological 2D horror game. In the distant future an astronaut gets stuck on Io, one of Jupiter’s moons. Inspired by The Very Pulse of the Machine from Love, Death & Robots. Currently on hold.

I design products at Lifetech Ti Mongolia, mostly on Tino — a superapp people in Ulaanbaatar use to get around and pay for things. Outside of that I build small apps for reasons that usually start as a joke, and games, which I am much slower at.
